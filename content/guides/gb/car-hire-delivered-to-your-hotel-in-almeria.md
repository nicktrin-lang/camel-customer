## What are the roads like around Almeria for UK drivers?

Almeria province is easy to drive once you're used to a couple of differences from home. You drive on the right, overtake on the left, and speed limits are in kilometres per hour, typically 120km/h on the A-7 motorway, 90-100km/h on main roads, and 30-50km/h in towns. The A-7 runs along the coast connecting Almeria city with Mojacar and Roquetas de Mar, and it's generally quiet compared with the AP-7 further west near Malaga. Away from the motorway, roads into the Tabernas desert or up towards the Alpujarras can be narrower and winding, so a smaller or mid-size car is often more practical than a large SUV. Parking in Almeria city centre often uses a blue zone (zona azul) pay-and-display system on weekdays, so check signage before you leave the car.

## What day trips can you do with a hire car from Almeria?

Having your own car delivered to your hotel opens up the whole province, far beyond what buses or taxis comfortably reach:

- Cabo de Gata-Nijar Natural Park, roughly 30 minutes from Almeria city, for salt flats, volcanic coastline and quiet beaches.
- The Tabernas desert and Mini Hollywood film sets, around 25 minutes away, where many classic westerns were filmed.
- Mojacar, about an hour up the coast, a whitewashed hill town with good beaches below it.
- The Alpujarras foothills, for a change from coast to mountain scenery, with villages like Laujar de Andarax within easy reach.
- Granada and the Sierra Nevada, a longer drive of around two hours, feasible as a full day out if you start early.
