# Long Term Car Hire in Benidorm: How Camel Global Delivers Your Car to You

Long term car hire in Benidorm means booking a car for an extended stay, typically two weeks or more, and having it brought straight to you rather than collecting it from a rental desk. With Camel Global, you compare offers from local car hire companies on camel-global.com, pick the one that suits you, and the car is met and handed over wherever you're arriving, usually Alicante Airport or your Benidorm hotel. For UK travellers staying a fortnight, a month, or longer on the Costa Blanca, this matters more than for a short trip. A longer hire means more time exposed to queues, more fuel decisions, and more chance something changes with your dates. Here's what to know before you book.

## Why does long term car hire in Benidorm work differently to a short break?

A longer hire changes the maths and the logistics:

- **Airport queues cost you more time proportionally.** Losing an hour at a rental desk stings less on a two-week trip than on a four-week one where you're likely running errands, doing school pickups if you live locally, or juggling work.
- **Fuel decisions repeat.** If you're topping up fuel yourself over several weeks, a fair, transparent fuel arrangement matters more than a one-off full tank policy.
- **Flexibility matters.** Long stays are more likely to shift, a delayed flight, an extended visit, a change of plan. Knowing your cancellation terms in advance avoids stress.
- **Local companies can be more competitive for longer bookings** because they're bidding directly for your custom rather than working from a single fixed rate card.

Camel Global lets local, approved operators around Benidorm and Alicante bid for your long term booking, so you can compare offers rather than defaulting to whichever desk is nearest the arrivals hall.

## What should I know about driving from Benidorm on a long term hire?

- **Toll motorway (AP-7):** The AP-7 running past Benidorm is a toll road; the parallel N-332 is free but slower and passes through more towns.
- **Town centre driving:** Benidorm's old town and seafront areas have narrow one-way systems and limited, often paid, parking. Many visitors on a long stay use the car mainly for trips out of town and rely on foot or local transport within the resort itself.
- **Licence and documents:** UK licence holders can drive in Spain; bring your driving licence, passport, and hire documents together at all times, since Spanish police can request them at roadside checks.
- **Speed limits:** Motorways are typically 120 km/h, single carriageways around 90-100 km/h, and urban areas 30-50 km/h, clearly signposted but worth knowing before you set off.
- **Long term parking:** If you're based in Benidorm for weeks, check whether your accommodation offers off-street parking, as street parking in central areas can be limited during peak season.

## What day trips suit a long term hire car from Benidorm?

A longer rental means you can spread day trips out rather than cramming them into a short break:

- **Altea:** A 20 minute drive north along the coast, known for its old town and marina.
- **Guadalest:** Around 40 minutes inland, a mountain village with a reservoir and castle ruins.
- **Alicante city:** About 45 minutes to an hour south, worth a full day for the castle, old town and seafront.
- **Calpe and the Peñón de Ifach:** Roughly 30 minutes north, good for a coastal walk and beaches with fewer crowds than central Benidorm.
- **Valencia:** Around 1.5 hours north, feasible as a longer day trip if you're hiring for several weeks and want to see the wider region.

## How does the fuel policy work on a long term hire?

Instead of the common approach of paying for a full tank upfront and losing whatever's left, Camel Global's model uses a refundable fuel deposit. You pay this alongside the hire cost when you book. When you return the car at the end of your long term hire, the deposit is refunded minus the cost of the fuel you actually used. This is worth understanding before you travel so you know what to expect at both ends of a multi-week booking.

## How do I avoid hidden fees on long term car hire in Benidorm?

- Check what's included in the quoted price before accepting a bid, hire cost and fuel deposit should both be clear.
- Confirm the delivery point (Alicante Airport, your hotel, or another address in Benidorm) at the time of booking.
- Read the cancellation terms; Camel Global offers free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup.
- Keep your booking receipt from the platform and ask the operator for a proper VAT invoice for your records, since these are two different documents.
- Pay through the platform rather than arranging separate payments directly with the operator, so refunds and the booking itself stay traceable.

## What happens if my flight to Alicante is delayed?

Delays happen, particularly on long haul or connecting UK routes into Alicante. Because meet and greet hire is arranged around your specific arrival details rather than a generic desk queue, it's important to give accurate flight information at booking and to contact the operator or platform promptly if your flight time changes, so the handover can be adjusted rather than missed.
