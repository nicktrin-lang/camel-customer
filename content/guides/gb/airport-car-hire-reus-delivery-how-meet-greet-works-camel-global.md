## What should UK drivers know about roads near Reus?

Spain drives on the right, which takes a short adjustment for UK drivers used to the left. Around Reus, the main things to expect are:

- The AP-7 motorway runs along the coast and is a toll road (autopista de peaje); the N-340 runs parallel and is toll-free but slower with more junctions and roundabouts.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, typically 120 km/h on motorways, 90 to 100 km/h on main roads, and 30 to 50 km/h in towns like Salou or Cambrils.
- Roundabouts are common on approach roads into resort towns; give way to traffic already on the roundabout.
- A valid UK driving licence is accepted for driving in Spain; you do not need an International Driving Permit for a standard UK licence.
- Spanish law requires you to carry your licence, and it is sensible to also carry your passport, while the car should have a reflective vest and warning triangles, which the operator will typically supply with the vehicle.

Parking in central Salou and Cambrils in peak summer can be tight, so many visitors are glad to have delivery and collection sorted at the hotel rather than worrying about where to leave a car overnight in a busy resort street.
