## What should UK drivers know about the roads around Almeria?

Spain drives on the right, and most UK visitors adjust quickly, but a few local specifics are worth knowing before you set off from Almeria:

- **The AP-7/A-7 corridor** runs along the coast and connects Almeria city to the Costa de Almeria resorts and, further afield, towards Murcia or Granada.
- **The N-340** is the older coastal road, slower but useful for shorter local hops between towns.
- **Roundabouts give priority to traffic already on them**, similar to the UK, but junction layouts inland can be tighter and less signposted than motorway exits.
- **Rural roads towards Tabernas and the Almanzora valley** can be narrow and winding, so a smaller car is often easier to handle than a large one if you're heading inland.
- **Parking in Almeria city centre** is mostly metered or in car parks; older streets can be narrow, so check your car's dimensions if you're staying centrally.
