## Driving the Green Coast and Beyond

The N-632 and the smaller coastal roads west of Avilés wind through fishing villages, cider country and clifftop viewpoints, and they're narrow, twisty and occasionally steep, quite unlike UK A-roads. Cudillero in particular has a tight, terraced layout with limited car access near the harbour, so having your own vehicle for the approach roads and then parking on the outskirts works far better than trying to manage a shuttle or coach connection. Further inland, the northern flank of the Picos de Europa around Cangas de Onís and the Lagos de Covadonga road involves genuine mountain driving: hairpins, single-track stretches and seasonal access restrictions in high summer. A well-suited, appropriately sized car matters more here than on a typical airport-to-hotel run, which is another reason to compare real options from local operators rather than accept whatever's left at a generic desk.
