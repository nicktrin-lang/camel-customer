## Driving Out of Almería Towards Cabo de Gata

The airport sits just southeast of Almería city, and the road to Cabo de Gata-Níjar Natural Park is short and simple, mostly local roads rather than motorway. This is one of the last stretches of undeveloped coastline in Spain, protected from the high-rise development you'll find further along the Costa, so the roads through the park are narrower, sometimes unpaved near the beaches, and best driven in daylight if you're heading to spots like Playa de los Genoveses or the lighthouse at Cabo de Gata itself. A smaller or mid-size car with reasonable ground clearance is a sensible choice here rather than something low-slung, since a few of the approach roads to the best beaches are gravel tracks.

## Heading Inland to the Tabernas Desert

In the other direction, the A-92 takes you north out of Almería towards Tabernas, Europe's only true desert and the location for countless Western films shot in the 1960s and 70s. The drive is easy motorway followed by a stretch of open road, with dramatic changes in scenery as the green coastal fringe gives way to ochre badlands within about half an hour. There's little shade and few services once you're off the main road, so it's worth setting off with a full tank and a bottle of water, and taking care with the heat if you're travelling in summer.
