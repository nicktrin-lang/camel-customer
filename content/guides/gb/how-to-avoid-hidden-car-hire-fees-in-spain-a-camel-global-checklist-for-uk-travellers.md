# How to Avoid Hidden Car Hire Fees in Spain: A Camel Global Checklist for UK Travellers

How to avoid hidden car hire fees in Spain comes down to five checks before you pay: confirm the fuel policy, understand the excess and deposit terms, see exactly what's included in the headline price, check whether delivery or airport pickup carries a separate charge, and know your cancellation rights if your flight is delayed. Booking through a platform that shows the full price upfront, and only charges you for the fuel you actually use, removes most of the surprises that catch UK travellers out at Spanish airports like Malaga, Alicante, Palma de Mallorca, Barcelona and Madrid.

## Why Do Hidden Fees Catch Out So Many UK Travellers in Spain?

Most unwelcome surprises happen because the headline price you see when browsing isn't the full price. Common traps at Spanish airport rental desks include: fuel policies that make you buy a full tank you won't finish, large credit card holds for the excess that aren't clearly explained beforehand, young driver or additional driver charges added at the counter, airport concession surcharges baked into the final bill, and upsell pressure for cover you may not need once you're standing at the desk after a long flight. None of these are illegal, but they're easy to miss when you're comparing prices online rather than reading every line of the terms.

## What Should You Check Before You Pay? A Pre-Booking Checklist

Before confirming any car hire in Spain, work through this list:

- **Fuel policy**: Is it full-to-full, full-to-empty, or a refundable deposit where you only pay for what you use?
- **Excess and deposit**: How much will be held on your card, and when is it released?
- **What's included**: Are child seats, a second driver, or an automatic gearbox included in the quoted price, or added later?
- **Delivery and location fees**: Is airport or hotel delivery already part of the price, or a separate line item?
- **Cancellation terms**: Can you cancel free of charge if your flight changes, and how close to pickup?
- **The invoice**: Will you receive a proper VAT invoice from the company supplying the car, not just a payment receipt?

If a company can't answer all six clearly before you book, treat that as a warning sign.

## Fuel Policy Explained: What's the Fair Way to Pay for Petrol?

The traditional full-to-empty policy means you buy a full tank at the start and return the car empty, so you pay for fuel you never burn. Full-to-full sounds fairer but means chasing a petrol station near the drop-off point with a flight to catch. Camel Global uses a third approach: you leave a refundable fuel deposit when you book, and after the car is returned you get back everything except the fuel you actually used. There's no full tank to overpay for and no last-minute petrol station panic before your flight home.

## Does Car Hire Have to Involve a Big Excess and Deposit Hold?

A large credit card hold for the excess is one of the most common complaints about airport car hire, and it's rarely obvious until you're at the desk. Camel Global's pitch is straightforward: no hidden fees and no unexpected excess taken from your credit card. You see the hire cost and the fuel deposit when you book, through the platform, so there's nothing sprung on you at handover. Payments are processed through Stripe, and any refunds due, such as the unused portion of your fuel deposit, are managed through the platform rather than left to a counter conversation in a second language.

## One-Way, Automatic, Family and Long-Term Car Hire in Spain: What to Check

Different trip types come with their own fee traps:

- **One-way car hire in Spain** (for example, flying into Barcelona and out of Madrid): confirm any one-way or drop-off charge is shown in the offer you're comparing, not added afterwards.
- **Automatic car hire**: automatics can be requested when you tell Camel Global what you need, so ask local operators to confirm the gearbox is exactly what's quoted.
- **Family car hire with child seats**: state the number and type of child seats needed upfront so it's reflected in the offers you receive, rather than negotiated at handover.
- **Long-term car hire**: for stays of several weeks, get the full price for the whole period plus the fuel deposit terms in writing before booking, since longer hires make any hidden daily extra add up fast.

## How to Avoid Hidden Car Hire Fees in Spain: The Short Version

- Read the fuel policy before the price, not after.
- Ask what the excess and deposit actually are, and when the hold is released.
- Check child seats, extra drivers and gearbox type are in the quote you're comparing, not added later.
- Confirm delivery to your airport or hotel is included in the price shown.
- Check the cancellation window in case your flight changes.
- Keep the VAT invoice from the company supplying the car, separate from your booking receipt.
