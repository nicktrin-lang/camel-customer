## Why the Ibiza Airport arrivals hall isn't where you want to spend your holiday

Ibiza Airport (IBZ) has a single terminal, but in peak summer months it handles a huge volume of charter and low-cost traffic from across the UK, including routes from Luton, Stansted, Birmingham and Edinburgh alongside the more established Gatwick and Manchester services. Arrivals can get congested quickly once several flights land close together, and the traditional car hire desks inside the terminal often see long queues forming exactly when everyone wants to get moving. If your flight lands mid-afternoon in July or August, that queue can eat into the first couple of hours of your holiday. Meet and greet car hire removes that step entirely: you arrange everything before you fly, and the car is brought to an agreed meeting point outside arrivals, so you can walk straight from baggage reclaim to your hire car.

## Driving in Ibiza: what UK visitors should know

The most important adjustment for UK drivers is that Spain drives on the right, which takes a little concentration in the first few miles, particularly at roundabouts on the ring road around Ibiza Town. From the airport, San Antonio is around a twenty-minute drive along the PM-803 and PM-802, Ibiza Town itself is closer still at roughly ten minutes, and Santa Eulalia on the east coast typically takes around twenty-five minutes via the C-733. Roads are generally well maintained, though narrow lanes near Es Vedra and some of the smaller cala roads in the north can be tight in high season traffic. Having your own car collected at the airport rather than relying on taxis or infrequent buses gives you far more flexibility to explore beyond the main resorts, including day trips to the ferry port at Ibiza Town for a crossing to Formentera.
