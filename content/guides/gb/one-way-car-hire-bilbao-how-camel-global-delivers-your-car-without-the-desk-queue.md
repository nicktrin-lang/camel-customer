## What Roads and Driving Conditions Should UK Travellers Expect Around Bilbao?

Spain drives on the right, so this is the main adjustment for UK drivers, especially at roundabouts and when overtaking. Around Bilbao itself:

- The city sits in a river valley with tunnels and elevated motorway sections (the AP-8 and A-8) connecting it to San Sebastian eastwards and Santander westwards.
- The AP-8 is a toll motorway; the parallel A-8 is toll-free but can be slower through built-up stretches.
- Bilbao's city centre has restricted low-emission zones in parts of the old town and Abando, so check with your operator whether your vehicle needs a permit if you plan to drive into the centre.
- Mountain roads inland towards Vitoria-Gasteiz or the Basque countryside are narrow and winding compared to UK A-roads, so allow extra time.

## Where Can I Drop Off a One Way Hire Car From Bilbao?

Common one way routes from Bilbao include:

- **San Sebastian** (roughly an hour east along the AP-8), popular for a coastal finish to a trip.

- **Vitoria-Gasteiz**, inland and quieter, good for travellers heading towards La Rioja wine country.
