## Driving Out of Girona Towards the Coast

Once you're on the road, Girona's position makes it a genuinely good base for exploring. The AP-7 motorway runs along the coast and connects easily to the C-31 and C-32 toll roads for the Costa Brava's resort towns, Palamos, Platja d'Aro and Tossa de Mar among them. If you're heading inland, Girona's medieval old city is only around twenty minutes' drive, with its cathedral steps and riverside houses well worth a detour before you continue. Toll roads are common in this part of Catalonia, so keep some coins or a card handy, and remember that speed limits are enforced strictly, including by average-speed cameras on some stretches of motorway.

## What UK Drivers Should Know Before Setting Off

Spain drives on the right, which takes most UK visitors a short while to adjust to, particularly at roundabouts. Your UK driving licence is valid for driving in Spain, though it's worth checking current requirements before you travel in case anything has changed. Parking in town centres like Girona or Palamos is often metered or restricted to residents in the old quarters, so look out for designated car parks on the edge of historic centres rather than trying to find a space on narrow medieval streets.
