## Driving in Madrid and on the roads around it: what UK visitors should know

Spain drives on the right, which takes most UK drivers a short adjustment period, especially at roundabouts and junctions. A few practical points for Madrid specifically:

- Madrid Central is a low emission zone in the city centre with restricted access for some vehicles. Check your operator's car meets the requirements if you plan to drive into the very centre.
- The M-30 and M-40 are Madrid's main ring roads, useful for getting around the city without cutting through the centre.
- Spain has both toll motorways (autopistas, marked AP) and free dual carriageways (autovías, marked A), so you can often choose a slower free route or a faster toll one.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, and fines for speeding or phone use while driving are enforced.
- Your UK driving licence is valid for hiring and driving in Spain; bring it along with your passport.
