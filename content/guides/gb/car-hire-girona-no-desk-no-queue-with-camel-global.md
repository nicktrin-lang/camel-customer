## Why does "no desk, no queue" matter at Girona Airport?

Girona-Costa Brava Airport is compact, but it's a hugely popular arrival point for UK travellers heading to the Costa Brava, and flights from the UK often land in clusters, particularly on Friday and Sunday evenings in peak season. That means rental desks can back up fast, with everyone from several flights queuing at the same time for keys, paperwork and upsells.

With meet and greet car hire, none of that applies to you. You've already compared offers and booked your car online before you travelled. When you land, collect your bags, and walk out, your car is brought to you rather than you trekking to a depot or waiting behind a dozen other arrivals at a counter.

## How does meet and greet car hire actually work in Girona?

The process is straightforward and happens mostly before you even leave the UK:

1. **Tell us what you need** - your dates, pickup point at Girona (or nearby hotel), and the type of car, via camel-global.com.
2. **Local operators bid for your booking** - approved Girona-area car hire companies send their offers.
3. **You pick an offer and book** - you pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit through the platform.
4. **Your car is delivered to you** - met and handed over at the time and place you agreed, whether that's the airport or your accommodation.
5. **You drive off** - straight into the Costa Brava or on to Girona city, no detour to a depot.
6. **You only pay for the fuel you used** - return the car and the rest of your fuel deposit is refunded.

## What should UK travellers know about Girona Airport arrivals?

Girona-Costa Brava Airport sits around 12km south of Girona city and is well connected to the AP-7 motorway, which runs along the coast towards Figueres and the French border in one direction and down towards Barcelona in the other. It's a smaller, simpler airport than Barcelona-El Prat, which is one reason meet and greet works so well here: there's less distance between the terminal and where you'll actually be driving.

If you're arriving on a low-cost carrier from a UK regional airport, landing times can vary flight to flight, so it helps that your meet and greet booking is arranged around your actual arrival rather than a fixed desk opening time.

## Driving in and around Girona: what to expect

Roads around Girona are generally well maintained. The AP-7 toll motorway is the fastest route along the coast and towards Barcelona or France, while free alternatives (the N-II and local roads) run parallel but slower. Girona's old town has narrow, largely pedestrianised streets, so if your hotel is central, agree your meet and greet drop-off and return point carefully, as some hotels aren't accessible by car right to the door.

## What are the best day trips from Girona with a hire car?

- **Costa Brava coastline** - towns like Tossa de Mar, Palamós and Cadaqués are 30 to 60 minutes away and best explored with your own car, since bus links between coastal villages can be limited.
- **Figueres** - around 40 minutes north via the AP-7, home to the Dalí Theatre-Museum.
- **Girona old town and cathedral** - if you're based on the coast, a short drive back into the city is worthwhile for the medieval quarter and the Onyar riverside houses.
- **French border towns** - the AP-7 makes a day trip across into France a realistic option from Girona.
