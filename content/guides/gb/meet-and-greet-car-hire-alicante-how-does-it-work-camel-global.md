Meet and greet car hire in Alicante means a local car hire company delivers your car to you at Alicante-Elche Airport (ALC), your hotel, or another agreed spot, rather than you queueing at a rental desk after landing. With Camel Global, you post your trip details, local Alicante operators bid for your booking, you choose the offer you like, pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, and your car is handed to you on arrival. You only settle up on the fuel you actually used when the car is collected at the end of your trip.

For UK travellers flying into Alicante, this matters because ALC handles a huge amount of British holiday traffic, especially in summer, and the rental desks can mean a long wait after a long flight. Skipping that queue and going straight to your car is often the biggest single time saver of the whole arrival process.

## How does meet and greet car hire work at Alicante Airport?

The process is straightforward and happens mostly before you even land:

1. **Tell Camel Global what you need.** On camel-global.com, enter your Alicante arrival details, dates, and the type of car (family car, small hatchback, automatic, and so on).
2. **Local operators bid on your booking.** Approved car hire companies operating around Alicante send their offers, so you're comparing real, competing prices rather than one fixed desk rate.
3. **You choose and book.** You pick the offer that suits you and pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, securely through the platform.
4. **Your car is delivered to you.** The operator meets you at the agreed time and place, typically at Alicante Airport, though delivery to your hotel along the Costa Blanca is also an option.
5. **You drive off.** No shuttle bus to an off-site depot, no standing in line behind a dozen other flights' worth of passengers.
6. **You settle fuel at the end.** When you return the car, you're only charged for the fuel you actually used, and the rest of your deposit is refunded.

## What should UK drivers know about roads around Alicante?

Driving in Spain means driving on the right, and Alicante's road network is generally easy for UK visitors once you're used to that switch.

- **The AP-7 motorway** runs along the coast and is the fastest way to reach Benidorm, Altea, Denia, or south towards Torrevieja and Murcia. It's a toll road in parts.
- **The N-332** is the free coastal alternative, slower but scenic, useful if you're not in a rush.
- **Roundabouts** are common and work the same way as in the UK, just approached from the opposite lane discipline.
- **Speed limits** are in kilometres per hour: typically 120 km/h on motorways, 90-100 km/h on main roads, and 30-50 km/h in towns.
- **Parking in Alicante city centre** is largely metered (zona azul) or in car parks; blue zone bays require a ticket displayed on the dashboard.

## What are good day trips from Alicante with a hire car?

Having your own car delivered on arrival opens up the whole Costa Blanca without depending on coach transfers:

- **Benidorm and Altea**, around 45 minutes north on the AP-7, for beaches and the old town views at Altea.
- **Guadalest**, roughly an hour inland, a mountain village with a reservoir and castle ruins.
- **Elche**, about 25 minutes south west, known for its palm groves and historic centre.
- **Murcia city**, around an hour south, for a change of pace from the coast.
- **Torrevieja and the Costa Blanca South salt lagoons**, roughly 45 minutes south, good for a slower beach day.

## How do you avoid hidden car hire fees in Alicante?

Hidden charges usually creep in through fuel policy, surprise excess holds on your card, or add-ons bundled in at the counter. With Camel Global, you see the offer from the local operator before you book, pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit upfront, and you're not left guessing what will land on your credit card statement afterwards. If you're comparing offers, check what's included (child seats, additional drivers, one way drop-off) before you commit, since these details are shown as part of the bid rather than added later at a desk.

## What if my flight to Alicante is delayed?

Because your car is met and handed over rather than collected from a fixed desk with fixed opening hours, it's worth giving your operator accurate flight details when you book so the delivery can be coordinated around your actual arrival time. If your flight time changes, update your booking details as soon as you know, so the local operator can adjust when and where they meet you.
