# Long Term Car Hire Girona: How Camel Global Delivers Your Car to You

Long term car hire in Girona means booking a car for an extended stay (typically a week or more) rather than a short weekend rental, and it works best when the car is delivered to you rather than collected from a rental desk. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, and only the fuel you actually use is deducted when the car is returned.

## What Counts as Long Term Car Hire in Girona?

There's no single legal definition, but in practice long term car hire in Girona usually means anything from ten days up to several months, aimed at travellers who need a car for an extended holiday, a working stay near the Costa Brava, or a slower trip covering Catalonia, the Pyrenees and beyond. For UK travellers, this is different from a quick weekend hire because:

- You're more exposed to fuel policy differences over a longer period, since a full-to-full mistake or a poor refund policy compounds over more litres.
- Vehicle condition and reliability matter more, since you're depending on the car for weeks rather than days.
- Flexibility on dates matters more, since longer trips are more likely to shift around flight changes or plans.

Camel Global's marketplace model suits longer hires well because you're comparing live offers from local operators for your specific dates, rather than accepting one fixed rate from a single counter.

## Girona's Roads: What UK Drivers Should Know

Driving in and around Girona is generally straightforward for UK visitors, but a few local points are worth knowing before a long stay:

- Traffic drives on the right, which takes most UK drivers a day or two to fully adjust to, particularly at roundabouts and junctions.
- The AP-7 motorway runs along the coast and is a toll road, useful for covering distance quickly between the Costa Brava, Girona city and Barcelona.
- The free alternative, the N-II, is slower but avoids tolls, and is a reasonable option if you're not in a rush.
- Girona's old town has narrow, partly pedestrianised streets, so a hire car is more useful for reaching the surrounding coast and countryside than for driving inside the historic centre itself.
- Parking in central Girona and in Costa Brava resort towns in peak season can be tight, so factor this in if your accommodation doesn't include off-street parking.

A long term hire really pays off in flexibility, since you can build up a rhythm of day trips rather than committing to one big itinerary. From Girona, popular options include:

- The Costa Brava coastline, including Tossa de Mar, Palamós and the walking trails around Cap de Creus.
- Cadaqués and Figueres, the latter home to the Dalí Theatre-Museum, both within a manageable drive.

If your plans involve finishing your trip somewhere other than Girona, ask local operators about one way car hire Girona options when you compare offers.

## How to Avoid Hidden Fees on a Long Term Booking

A few habits help keep a long term Girona hire straightforward:

- Compare the full offer, not just the headline hire price, including what the fuel deposit covers.
- Check what's included for extras like child seats or additional drivers before you book, rather than adding them later.
- Keep your booking confirmation and receipt from the platform, and ask the operator for their invoice for your records.
- Use the free cancellation window if your travel dates change, rather than trying to renegotiate after booking.
- Confirm your delivery point (airport, hotel or address) clearly when you book, especially if you're arriving on a delayed flight.
