# One Way Car Hire Benidorm: How Does It Work with Camel Global?

One way car hire in Benidorm means picking up your car in Benidorm and dropping it off somewhere else, such as another airport or resort town, rather than returning it to the same spot. ## Why would a UK traveller want one way car hire in Benidorm?

Benidorm sits on the Costa Blanca, and plenty of UK holidaymakers build a trip around more than one base. Typical reasons for wanting a one way hire include:

- Flying into Alicante but flying home from a different airport after touring the coast.
- Starting in Benidorm and finishing in Valencia, Murcia, or further along the Costa Blanca.
- Combining a beach stay in Benidorm with a city break elsewhere, without doubling back.
- Picking up near your hotel and dropping off near a different accommodation later in the trip.

Because Camel Global works with local operators rather than a single fixed fleet, it's worth stating your route clearly (pickup point, drop-off point, dates) when you submit your request, so operators bidding on your booking can confirm whether a one way trip is possible for your dates and quote accordingly.

## Which airport do I land at, and how does delivery work?

Most UK flights to Benidorm land at Alicante-Elche Airport (ALC), roughly 50 kilometres south of the resort. There's no dedicated Benidorm airport, so Alicante is the standard gateway. With meet and greet car hire, you don't collect your car from a depot desk after baggage reclaim. Instead:

1. You land at Alicante and clear arrivals as normal.
2. Your chosen local operator meets you at an agreed point, either at the airport, your Benidorm hotel, or another address you've specified.
3. They hand over the car, you check it over together, and you're on your way.
4. At the end of your hire, the car is collected from wherever you agree, which for a one way booking may be a different town or airport entirely.

This is the core idea behind car hire delivered to Alicante airport: no shuttle bus to an off-site compound, no long counter, and no wasted holiday time standing in a queue.

## What should UK drivers know about roads around Benidorm?

Driving in Spain differs from the UK in a few practical ways worth planning for before you set off from Benidorm:

- Drive on the right, and remember this especially at roundabouts and junctions after a long flight.
- The AP-7 motorway runs along the coast and is the quickest route towards Valencia to the north or Alicante and Murcia to the south, useful for one way routes.
- Some motorway stretches carry a toll, so keep a card handy for peaje booths.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, and Benidorm's town centre has narrow one-way streets, so a smaller car is often easier to park near the seafront and Poniente or Levante beaches.
- Fuel stations are plentiful along the N-332 and AP-7, so route planning for a one way drop-off is straightforward.
