## How does queue-skipping actually work at Girona Airport?

Instead of walking from baggage reclaim to a row of rental desks, you arrange everything before you fly. Here's the sequence:

1. You tell Camel Global your dates, the kind of car you want, and where you're landing, for example Girona Airport.
2. Local, approved car hire companies in the area send back competing offers.
3. You compare and pick the one that suits you, then pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit through the platform.
4. A representative from the operator meets you at the arranged time and place, hands over the keys, and you're on the road.
5. When you return the car, you get back whatever's left of your fuel deposit after the fuel you actually used is accounted for.

There's no desk to find and no separate queue behind other arriving passengers all trying to collect a car at once.

## What actually happens when you land at Girona Airport?

## Driving from Girona: what UK visitors should know

Girona sits close to the AP-7 motorway, which links up towards the French border and down towards Barcelona, making it a practical base for exploring Costa Brava. Spanish motorways use tolls on some stretches, speed limits are in kilometres per hour, and drink-drive limits are stricter than in the UK. Roundabouts give priority to traffic already on them, much as at home, but junctions and slip roads can be signed differently, so it's worth glancing at a route before setting off rather than relying purely on satnav in the moment.
