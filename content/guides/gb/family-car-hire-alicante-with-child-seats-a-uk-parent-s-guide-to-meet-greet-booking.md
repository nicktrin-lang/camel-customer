## What does Spanish law actually require for child seats?

Spanish traffic law requires that any child under 135cm in height uses a homologated child restraint system appropriate to their height and weight, fitted in the rear seats of the car. This applies to UK visitors driving in Spain exactly as it applies to residents. The broad groups are:

- **Infant seats (rear-facing):** for babies from birth, typically used until around 13kg or when the child outgrows the seat by height, whichever comes first.
- **Toddler seats:** forward or rear-facing, generally covering roughly 9 to 18kg.
- **High-back booster seats:** for older children, generally from around 15kg up to the 135cm height threshold.
- **Booster cushions:** for the upper end of that weight and height range, used with the adult seatbelt.

Children under 12 are generally not permitted in the front passenger seat unless the rear seats are already occupied by other children under 135cm, the car has no rear seats, or another specific exemption applies, and if a rear-facing seat is used in the front, the airbag must be deactivated. Because these rules are based on height and weight rather than age alone, always book the seat category that matches your child's current measurements, not just their birthday.
