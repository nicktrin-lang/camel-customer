# One Way Car Hire Murcia: Meet & Greet Delivery With Camel Global

One way car hire in Murcia means picking up your car in one location, such as Región de Murcia International Airport, and dropping it off somewhere else at the end of your trip, rather than returning it to the same spot. With Camel Global, you request your dates, route and car type once, local car hire companies in the Murcia area bid for your booking, and the operator you choose delivers the car to you at the airport, your hotel, or wherever you land, with no rental desk and no queue to deal with first.

## What Is One Way Car Hire and How Does It Work in Murcia?

One way hire is simply a rental where the pickup and drop-off points differ, for example collecting at Región de Murcia International Airport (RMU, sometimes called Corvera Airport) and leaving the car at another airport, a train station, or a different town along the Costa Cálida. On Camel Global, the process is the same as a standard booking, just with a different drop-off location:

1. Enter your route, dates, and car requirements on camel-global.com, including where you want to pick up and drop off.
2. Approved local operators covering that route submit competing offers.
3. You compare and pick the deal that suits you, then [book your car hire](https://www.camel-global.com/book) and pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit.
4. The car is delivered and handed over, meet and greet style, at the time and place agreed.
5. You drop the car off at the agreed end point and get back whatever fuel deposit you didn't use.

Not every operator covers every one-way route, so availability and any one-way charge depend on which company bids on your specific request. It's worth submitting your exact route early so operators have time to respond.

## Where Do I Land, and How Do I Get My Car in Murcia?

Most UK travellers to the Murcia region arrive at Región de Murcia International Airport, which handles the scheduled flights that used to route through the smaller, now-closed San Javier airport. Instead of collecting a shuttle bus to an off-site depot after a long flight, meet and greet car hire brings the car to you at the terminal, so you go from baggage reclaim straight to your car. This matters most after an evening arrival or a delayed flight, when nobody wants to queue at a rental desk or wait for a transfer bus.

## Driving in the Murcia Region: What UK Visitors Should Know

Driving in Spain is on the right, and the Murcia region has a mix of motorways (autovías, generally toll-free around Murcia), dual carriageways and smaller rural roads heading into areas like Sierra Espuña. A few practical points for UK drivers:

- You'll need your UK driving licence; check current requirements for an International Driving Permit before you travel, as rules can change.
- Spanish law requires you to carry your licence, and it's sensible to carry ID, at all times while driving.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, and fines for speeding or phone use while driving are enforced.
- Roundabouts (rotondas) are common in Murcia city and along the coast; traffic already on the roundabout has priority.
- Summer heat is intense inland, so check your air conditioning and keep water in the car for longer drives.

## Best Day Trips From Murcia With a Hire Car

Having your own car in Murcia opens up the wider region well beyond the city itself:

- **Cartagena**: a historic port city around 45 minutes from Murcia city, with Roman ruins and a naval museum.
- **La Manga and the Mar Menor**: Europe's largest saltwater lagoon, popular for watersports, roughly 30 to 45 minutes from the airport depending on your route.
- **Lorca**: a castle town inland, good for a half-day visit.
- **Sierra Espuña Regional Park**: hiking and cooler air in summer, less than an hour from Murcia city.
- **Costa Cálida beaches**: numerous smaller coves and towns south of Cartagena, best reached with your own transport since public routes are limited.
