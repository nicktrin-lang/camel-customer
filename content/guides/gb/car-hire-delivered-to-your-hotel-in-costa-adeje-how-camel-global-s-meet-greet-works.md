# Car Hire Delivered to Your Hotel in Costa Adeje: How Camel Global's Meet & Greet Works

Car hire delivered to your hotel in Costa Adeje means skipping the rental desk at Tenerife South Airport altogether: you land, clear arrivals, get to your hotel in Playa de las Américas, Playa del Duque or La Caleta, and a local operator meets you there with the car already booked through Camel Global. You compare offers from approved local companies, pick one, and pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, getting back whatever fuel you didn't use when the car is collected.

## How does car hire delivered to your hotel in Costa Adeje actually work?

You tell Camel Global your dates, the type of car you need (family car with child seats, automatic, or something for a longer stay) and where you're staying in Costa Adeje. Local car hire companies then bid for your booking with their own prices. You choose the offer that suits you and book online. Instead of collecting the car at Tenerife South Airport, you arrange for it to be delivered to your accommodation once you've landed, checked in, and dropped your bags.

The steps in short:

1. Submit your destination (Costa Adeje), dates and car requirements on camel-global.com.
2. Compare offers from local operators competing for your booking.
3. Book and pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit.
4. Agree a delivery time and place, typically your hotel or apartment reception.
5. Meet the driver, check the car, and drive off, no depot visit involved.
6. Return the car at the agreed time and get back whatever fuel deposit you didn't use.

## Why skip the Tenerife South Airport rental desk after landing?

Tenerife South Airport (TFS), near El Médano, is roughly 20 to 25 minutes from Costa Adeje via the TF-1 motorway, depending on traffic and which part of Costa Adeje you're heading to (La Caleta sits slightly further west than Playa de las Américas). Rental desks at the airport can mean queuing behind other arriving flights, a shuttle bus to an off-site depot, and paperwork before you even see the car. If you're arriving tired after a UK flight, that's time you could spend getting to your hotel and onto the beach instead.

With delivery to your hotel, you take a taxi, transfer or bus straight from the airport, check in, and only deal with the car once you're settled. There's no desk, no queue, and no separate trip to a rental depot.

## How do you time delivery after check-in in Costa Adeje?

Most travellers arriving at Tenerife South Airport allow around 45 to 60 minutes for baggage claim and the transfer to Costa Adeje, then check-in at their hotel or apartment. A sensible approach is to book delivery for an hour or two after your expected hotel check-in time, giving you a buffer to unpack and settle before the driver arrives. If your flight is delayed, let the operator know your updated arrival window so the handover can be adjusted rather than missed. Because the car comes to you rather than waiting on a rental forecourt, delivery timing is built around your actual schedule, not a fixed counter slot.

## What documents does the driver need on-site?

When the operator meets you at your Costa Adeje accommodation, be ready with:

- A valid full driving licence (UK licences are accepted for hire in Spain; check current validity requirements before you travel).
- Your passport or photo ID.
- The booking confirmation from Camel Global.
- A payment card in the lead driver's name for the fuel deposit.

The driver will usually walk through the car with you, noting existing marks and confirming fuel level, before handing over the keys and any local paperwork from the hire company.

## What about fuel, deposits and hidden fees?

Camel Global works on a refundable fuel deposit rather than a prepaid full tank. You pay the hire cost plus the deposit when you book, and after the car is returned, you get back everything except the fuel you actually used during your Costa Adeje stay. There are no hidden extras added on top and no surprise excess charged to your credit card beyond what was agreed when you booked. The company you're matched with issues the proper hire paperwork, including a VAT invoice for the hire itself.

## Driving in and around Costa Adeje: what UK visitors should know

Spain drives on the right, which takes some adjusting for UK drivers, especially at roundabouts on the TF-1 motorway between the airport and Costa Adeje. Speed limits are posted in kilometres per hour. Parking in central Playa de las Américas can be tight in high season, so check whether your hotel offers parking before you commit to a car for your whole stay. If you're planning trips further afield, such as to Los Cristianos, Vilaflor or up towards Teide, an automatic car hire or a slightly larger family car with child seats can make mountain roads and roundabouts easier to manage. One way car hire and long term car hire options are also worth requesting if your itinerary doesn't end back in Costa Adeje.
