# What Happens to My Car Hire in Spain If My Flight Is Delayed? Camel Global Explains

If your flight to Spain is delayed, you simply update your pickup time with the local operator handling your booking, and your car is still delivered to you once you land, at the airport, your hotel, or wherever you've arranged. With Camel Global's meet & greet model there's no rental desk queue to rush for and no shuttle bus timetable to catch, so a delay is far less stressful than it would be with a traditional airport rental counter. The key is communication: let the operator know as soon as you have a new landing time, using your flight number, so the handover can be adjusted.

## Why does a flight delay matter less with meet and greet car hire?

With a conventional airport rental desk, a delay can mean losing your place in a queue, missing a shuttle bus to an off-site depot, or arriving to find the counter closed for the night. Meet & greet car hire in Spain works differently because the car comes to you rather than the other way round.

- You agree a pickup time and location when you book through camel-global.com.
- If your flight to Malaga, Alicante, Palma, Barcelona, Madrid or any other Spanish airport is delayed, you contact the operator to update that time.
- The handover still happens at the airport, your hotel, or your holiday address, once you've actually arrived.
- There's no depot bus to miss and no counter that shuts before you get through immigration and baggage reclaim.

This is one of the practical reasons people ask whether meet and greet car hire is worth it: it removes the single biggest point of friction that flight delays create, which is timing your arrival to a fixed, staffed desk.

## What should I do the moment I know my flight is delayed?

1. Check your new estimated landing time as soon as the airline confirms it.
2. Message the local operator (details are in your booking confirmation) with your flight number and revised time.
3. Confirm the new meeting point, especially if you're being met inside the terminal, at arrivals, or at a hotel later that evening.
4. Keep your phone charged and reachable. Spanish mobile networks work fine at all major airports, but a dead phone makes coordination difficult.
5. If the delay pushes your arrival very late, ask the operator to confirm they can still deliver that night rather than assuming it automatically.

Doing this early, rather than after you land, gives the operator time to adjust their schedule around your new arrival.

## Meet and greet vs airport rental desk: how delays play out

| Situation | Meet & greet car hire | Traditional rental desk |
|---|---|---|
| Flight delayed 1-2 hours | Update pickup time with operator; car still delivered | Desk staff may still be there, but you queue behind other delayed passengers |
| Flight delayed overnight | Handover rearranged for your actual arrival | Desk may be closed; you may need a taxi or hotel until it reopens |
| Landing very late | Delivery to hotel or address can be discussed in advance | Off-site depot shuttle may have stopped running |
| Communicating a change | Direct message to the operator via your booking | Often only a general customer service line |

This is the practical difference between meet and greet car hire and an airport rental desk when things don't go to plan: one adapts to your actual arrival time, the other relies on a fixed counter schedule.

## What if my flight is delayed so much I need to change the date entirely?

If a delay is severe enough to shift your travel by a day or more, or your flight is cancelled outright, Camel Global bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the agreed pickup time. If you're already inside that 48-hour window when the delay happens, contact the operator directly through the platform to explain the situation and ask about rearranging rather than cancelling. Because you're dealing with a specific local company rather than a large anonymous counter, it's usually easier to have a real conversation about options.

## Does a flight delay affect my fuel deposit or the price I agreed?

No. The price you accepted when you booked, and the refundable fuel deposit you pay alongside it, aren't affected by a delay itself. You still pay only for the fuel you actually use once you return the car, and the rest of the deposit is refunded afterwards. A delay changes the timing of the handover, not the commercial terms you agreed with the operator.

## Is it safe to rely on a platform I haven't used before, especially if my plans change?

Camel Global is a live platform processing real bookings, with payments handled securely through Stripe, and it supports multiple currencies and six languages, so UK travellers booking a Spanish hire car aren't dealing with an unfamiliar checkout in an unfamiliar language. The company that actually delivers your car is a local, approved operator, not Camel Global itself. That operator supplies the vehicle and issues your VAT invoice; the platform's job is to let operators bid for your booking, take secure payment, and manage the refund of your fuel deposit afterwards. Your booking confirmation isn't itself a VAT invoice, but a proper invoice comes from the operator.

## What should UK drivers know about roads in Spain once they've collected the car?

Spain drives on the right, and this catches out UK visitors most often at roundabouts, where traffic flows anticlockwise and you give way to vehicles already on the roundabout from your left. Speed limits are posted in kilometres per hour, not miles, and motorways (autopistas and autovías) often have tolls on the autopista sections, payable by card or cash at booths. City driving in places like Barcelona, Madrid or Malaga can be dense, so many visitors prefer collecting the car once they're ready to head out of town rather than driving straight from the terminal into rush-hour traffic, which is another reason a flexible, delivered handover suits arrivals better than a fixed-time desk pickup.

## FAQ

### What happens to my car hire if my flight is delayed in Spain?

You update the local operator with your new arrival time and flight number, and the meet & greet handover is rearranged around your actual landing, whether that's at the airport, your hotel, or another agreed address. The price and fuel deposit terms you already agreed stay the same.

### Will I be charged extra if my flight lands late at night?

Camel Global's published terms don't include hidden fees, and a late landing on its own doesn't change the agreed hire price. If your delay is significant, message the operator as early as possible so they can confirm the handover is still workable at that hour.

### Can I cancel my car hire if my flight is cancelled?

Yes, bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the agreed pickup time. If your flight cancellation happens closer to pickup than that, contact the operator directly through the platform to discuss rearranging rather than losing the booking.

### Is meet and greet car hire worth it compared with an airport rental desk?

For many travellers, yes, particularly around delays. It removes the need to reach a physical counter within opening hours or catch a depot shuttle, since the car is delivered to you once you've actually arrived, and local operators compete on price rather than you defaulting to whichever desk is nearest.

### Do I need to bring anything extra in case of a delay?

Just keep your flight number and booking confirmation handy, and make sure you can be reached by phone or message once you land. That's normally all the operator needs to adjust your handover time.
