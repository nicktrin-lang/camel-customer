## What happens when you land at Bilbao Airport?

Bilbao Airport (BIO) sits around 12 kilometres north of the city centre, and it's the main gateway for UK travellers heading into the Basque Country. With a meet and greet car hire booking, you skip the rental desk queue entirely: instead of finding the counter, filling in paperwork, and waiting behind other arrivals, you're met at an agreed point, often airport arrivals, with the car ready to go. This matters more on a long term hire, since you don't want the first hours of a multi-week stay eaten up by desk formalities, or a full tank of fuel to sort out before you've even left the terminal.

## What should UK drivers know about roads around Bilbao?

Driving in Spain means driving on the right, and Bilbao's road network is generally straightforward for visitors used to UK motorways. The AP-8 runs east along the coast towards San Sebastián and the French border, while the AP-68 heads south towards La Rioja and onward to Zaragoza and Madrid. Both are toll motorways (autopistas), so it's worth budgeting for tolls on longer road trips, alongside the free A-8 dual carriageway alternative that runs a similar route more slowly. Within Bilbao itself, much of the old town and riverside area has restricted or metered parking, and some zones require a resident permit, so a hotel with parking or a car park near your accommodation is worth checking before you arrive. Speed limits and give-way rules follow standard Spanish signage, and UK driving licences are accepted for tourists.

## Best day trips from Bilbao with a long term hire car

A longer hire is really where a car earns its keep, since you can explore well beyond the city at your own pace:

- **La Rioja wine country**, under an hour south via the AP-68, for vineyards and bodegas around Haro and Laguardia.

- **The Picos de Europa**, further south, a good full-day trip for mountain scenery if your hire runs long enough to fit it in.

One-way car hire Bilbao options are also worth asking about if your trip starts in Bilbao but finishes elsewhere, since some operators can arrange drop-off at a different location.

## How do you avoid hidden fees on a long term booking?

The biggest risks with any extended hire are unclear fuel policies, unexpected excess holds on your card, and add-on charges that only appear at the desk. To avoid them:

- Check the fuel policy before you book, and understand whether it's a refundable deposit or a pre-paid tank.
- Confirm what excess applies and whether it's held against your credit card.
- Ask what's included (mileage, insurance, breakdown cover) rather than assuming.
- Get everything in writing, and keep the booking confirmation alongside the operator's VAT invoice.

On Camel Global, offers are transparent from local operators, you pay securely through the platform, and refunds on the fuel deposit are managed through the platform once the car is returned.
