If you're flying from the UK to Spain and want car hire with no desk and no queue, meet and greet car hire delivers the vehicle to you at the airport, your hotel, or wherever you land, instead of sending you to a rental counter after your flight. Camel Global is a marketplace where local Spanish car hire companies bid for your booking, so you compare offers and pick one before you even touch down. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, the car is handed to you where you arrive, and any free cancellation window applies if your plans change.

## How does meet and greet car hire actually work for UK travellers landing in Spain?

The process is designed to remove the two things that make airport car hire frustrating: the shuttle bus to an off-site depot, and the queue at a rental desk.

1. **Tell us what you need** - your Spanish arrival airport (Malaga, Alicante, Palma de Mallorca, Faro if you're crossing into the Algarve, or wherever you're landing), your dates, and the type of car.
2. **Local operators bid for your booking** - approved Spanish car hire companies in that area send back their offers through camel-global.com.
3. **Pick the offer and book** - you pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, processed securely through Stripe.
4. **Your car is delivered to you** - met and handed over at the time and place you agreed, whether that's the arrivals area, your hotel, or an apartment.
5. **Drive** - no shuttle bus, no depot trip.
6. **Return the car and get your unused fuel deposit back** - the operator refunds whatever you didn't use.

Because the platform supports multiple currencies and six languages, UK travellers can request and compare offers in a way that's straightforward whether you're booking from home or on the move.

## What should UK drivers know about driving in Spain?

Spain drives on the right, which is the main adjustment for UK drivers used to driving on the left. A few practical points:

- **Documents**: bring your valid UK driving licence. An International Driving Permit is not usually needed for a standard UK licence, but check current requirements before you travel, as rules can change.
- **Motorways (autopistas)**: many are toll roads (peajes), paid at booths as you go; free alternatives (autovías) usually run alongside them but can be slower.
- **Speed limits**: typically 120 km/h on motorways, 90-100 km/h on other main roads, and 50 km/h (often 30 km/h) in towns and cities, all clearly signed in kilometres.
- **Roundabouts**: give way to traffic already on the roundabout, same principle as the UK, just approached from the opposite lane position.
- **Fines**: on-the-spot fines exist for some offences, and speed cameras are common on motorways.
- **Parking**: blue zone (zona azul) parking requires a paid ticket displayed in the car; check local signage in city centres.

## How does the fuel policy work with Camel Global?

Instead of buying a full tank upfront or worrying about returning the car exactly full, you leave a refundable fuel deposit when you book. When you return the car, the operator refunds the deposit minus the cost of the fuel you actually used. This avoids the two common frustrations of traditional fuel policies: paying for fuel you never burn, or the stress of hunting for a petrol station right before drop-off.

One of the biggest concerns with car hire abroad is discovering charges you didn't agree to, particularly a large excess held against your card. With Camel Global, you see and compare offers from local operators before booking, pay through the platform via Stripe, and the operator issues a proper invoice for your hire. Because pricing is set out upfront through the bidding process rather than added to at a desk, there's less room for surprise add-ons after you've landed.

## Who actually turns up with the car, and can I trust them?

The car is delivered by the local Spanish operator who won your booking through the bidding process, not by Camel Global itself. Camel Global is the platform that connects you with approved operators, processes payment securely through Stripe, and manages the booking and refund of your fuel deposit. The operator is the one who supplies the vehicle, meets you at the agreed location, and issues your invoice.

Delays happen, and meet and greet arrangements are built around your actual arrival rather than a fixed desk closing time. Always share your flight details when booking so the operator can track your arrival and adjust the handover time accordingly, and contact the operator directly if you know your flight is significantly delayed. If your plans change before you travel, free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup gives you flexibility without penalty.
