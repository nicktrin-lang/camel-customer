This suits UK parents landing at Mahón (MAH) after a flight with tired children who want to be driving to their villa or hotel within minutes, not standing in a counter queue fitting seats themselves in a car park.

The process runs in five simple steps:

4. **Meet and greet at Mahón.** Your car, with seats correctly installed, is delivered and handed to you at arrivals, at your hotel, or wherever you specify.
5. **Drive off, then settle up.** Enjoy your holiday, and when you return the car, you get back whatever you did not use of your fuel deposit.

Because the booking, the offers and the payment all happen on one platform, you can see exactly what you are agreeing to before you fly, rather than being upsold extras at a counter after landing.

## Why choose meet & greet over the airport rental desk in Menorca?

Mahón Airport is small compared with major UK airports, but arrivals can still mean a walk to a rental village or a wait for a transfer bus if you go the traditional route. With meet & greet, the car is brought to you at arrivals instead. That matters most with children: no juggling luggage and a toddler while queuing at a desk, no waiting for staff to locate and fit a car seat, no separate bus journey with car seats and bags in tow.

Meet & greet also means local Menorcan operators compete for your booking rather than you defaulting to whichever big-brand counter has the shortest queue. You see multiple offers side by side and choose based on price, car type and the seats on offer.

## What child seats can I request for Menorca car hire?

When you submit your request, specify the ages (or approximate weights) of each child travelling, since Spanish law categorises restraints by size rather than by simple age bands. Typical categories requested by UK families include:

- Rear-facing infant carriers for babies
- Forward-facing toddler seats
- High-back boosters for older children
- Multiple seats for families with more than one child

Local operators fit the seats before the car is delivered, so you are not adjusting straps in the airport car park with jet lag setting in. If your family needs an unusual combination, such as three seats across one row, mention this in your request so operators can confirm which vehicle sizes can accommodate it.

## What does it cost, and what happens with fuel?

You pay the hire cost agreed with your chosen local operator, plus a refundable fuel deposit. Camel Global does not set a fixed price; instead, competing offers from local companies give you a genuine comparison so you can judge value for your dates and car type. On fuel, you are not required to buy a full tank upfront and hope you use it all: you leave a deposit, and after the car is returned you get back everything except the fuel you actually used.

## Driving in Menorca with kids: roads, tolls and parking

Menorca is compact, with the Me-1 running roughly east to west between Mahón (Maó) and Ciutadella, the island's two main towns. There are no motorway-style tolls on this route, and most family destinations, from Cala Galdana to Fornells, are within an hour's drive of the airport. Roads narrow considerably towards beaches and rural resorts, so a car sized sensibly for your child seats and luggage, rather than the largest available, often makes for an easier holiday. Parking at popular beaches fills up in peak summer, so an earlier start is worth planning with young children.

## What day trips work well from Mahón with a family car?

Having your own car collected straight at arrivals means you are not losing a day to logistics. Families commonly use their hire car for:

- Mahón's harbour and old town, a short drive from the airport
- The beaches of the south coast, such as Son Bou and Cala en Porter
- Ciutadella, roughly 45 minutes across the island
- Fornells and the quieter north coast for calmer waters with younger children

## What happens if my flight is delayed?

Meet & greet handovers are arranged for the time and place you agree with your chosen local operator, and communication about your booking happens through the platform. If your arrival time changes, this is something to flag as early as possible with the operator handling your booking so the handover can be adjusted, rather than assuming a fixed desk will simply wait.
