# Is Meet and Greet Car Hire Worth It for UK Travellers Heading to Spain? Camel Global's Honest Breakdown

For most UK travellers flying into Spain, meet and greet car hire is worth it: it removes the shuttle bus and rental desk queue, gets you on the road faster after a long flight, and lets local operators compete for your booking through a platform like Camel Global instead of you defaulting to whichever desk has the shortest line. It matters most for families with luggage and tired children, business travellers with tight schedules, and anyone who has landed at Malaga, Alicante or Palma late in the evening only to queue for 45 minutes at a rental counter. It matters less if you're landing early with no time pressure, want to browse a large fleet in person, or need a very specific one-way deal only offered at one particular desk.

## How Does Meet and Greet Car Hire Work in Spain?

The process is designed to remove the two things travellers dislike most about airport car hire: the counter and the courtesy bus to an off-site depot.

1. **Tell us what you need.** On camel-global.com, you enter your Spanish airport or destination, your dates, and the type of car (family car, automatic, one way, long term, whatever fits your trip).
2. **Local operators bid for your booking.** Approved car hire companies covering that area send you their offers, so you can compare rather than accept the first price shown.
3. **You pick an offer and book.** You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit through the platform.
4. **Your car is delivered to you.** Handover happens at the time and place you agreed, whether that's the airport arrivals area, your hotel, or a villa.
5. **You drive off.** No shuttle bus, no walking to a rental floor, no separate depot visit.
6. **You get your fuel deposit balance back.** When you return the car, whatever you didn't use is refunded, rather than losing a full pre-paid tank.

This is the core difference from a traditional desk: the car comes to you, and the paperwork and payment happen through the platform before you even land.

## Is It Worth It If You're Short on Time?

This is where meet and greet earns its keep. UK flights into Spain, particularly to Malaga, Alicante, Barcelona-El Prat, Palma de Mallorca and Girona, are often scheduled to land in the early evening or late at night during peak season. Airport rental desks in these periods regularly see queues after several flights land within the same hour. A meet and greet handover means you're not competing with three other arriving flights for the same three staff members at a counter. If your holiday time is limited, whether it's a long weekend or a school-holiday week, the minutes saved at both ends of the trip add up.

## What About the Fuel Policy? Will I Overpay?

A common frustration with car hire is fuel: pay upfront for a full tank you won't finish, or worry about finding a petrol station near the airport before returning the car. Camel Global's model works differently. You leave a refundable fuel deposit when you book, and after your trip, the balance beyond the fuel you actually used is refunded. There's no full-tank purchase built into the price, so you're not paying in advance for petrol you never burn.

## What Happens If My Flight Is Delayed?

Because the car is delivered to an agreed time and place rather than sitting on a depot forecourt waiting for you, it's worth notifying your chosen local operator through the platform as soon as you know your flight is running late. The handover is arranged around your actual arrival rather than a fixed counter opening hours, which is one of the practical advantages of the meet and greet model over queuing at a desk with a cut-off time.

## When Might the Traditional Rental Desk Still Make Sense?

This isn't a one-size-fits-all answer, and a balanced look means acknowledging where a desk can still work well:

- **You want to see the car before committing** and prefer to inspect the physical fleet in person rather than book based on a description.
- **You're arriving with hours to spare** and time pressure isn't a factor, so a queue is more of an inconvenience than a problem.
- **You need to change your booking on the spot** and prefer face-to-face negotiation over managing changes through a platform.

For everyone else, particularly UK travellers landing tired after a flight with young children, luggage, or an evening arrival, delivery to you removes a genuinely stressful step from the journey.

## How Do I Avoid Hidden Car Hire Fees in Spain?

Hidden fees are usually the result of one-off desk upsells or unclear fuel rules. To avoid them:

- Compare the full offer, not just the headline rate, before you accept a bid.
- Understand the fuel policy before booking, specifically whether it's a refundable deposit or a pre-paid full tank.
- Check cancellation terms. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup gives you room to change plans without penalty.
- Ask for a proper invoice for your hire, issued by the local company that supplied the car.
