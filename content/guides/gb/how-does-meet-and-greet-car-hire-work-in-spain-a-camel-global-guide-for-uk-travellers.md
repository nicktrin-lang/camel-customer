# How Does Meet and Greet Car Hire Work in Spain? A Camel Global Guide for UK Travellers

Meet and greet car hire in Spain works by cutting out the rental desk entirely: you book online before you fly, local car hire companies compete for your booking, and your chosen car is driven to arrivals and handed to you directly, usually within a few minutes' walk of the terminal. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, then get any unused fuel deposit back when you return the car. There's no shuttle bus to a depot, no counter queue, and no walking past twenty desks trying to find yours.

For UK travellers who've landed in Malaga, Alicante, Palma de Mallorca or Barcelona after a delayed flight, only to queue for 45 minutes behind ten other confused holidaymakers, this is the part of the trip meet and greet hire is built to fix.

## What Happens From Booking to Handover, Step by Step?

Here's the actual sequence, from the moment you decide you need a car in Spain to driving away from the airport:

1. **You submit your request.** On camel-global.com you enter your Spanish destination, arrival and departure dates, and the type of car you need (compact, automatic, family car with child seats, and so on).
2. **Local operators bid on your booking.** Approved car hire companies in that part of Spain, whether it's the Costa del Sol, the Costa Blanca, Mallorca or Catalonia, send you their offers.
3. **You compare and choose.** You see the price upfront and pick the offer that suits you, then book and pay securely through the platform.
4. **You arrange the handover point.** Typically this is Arrivals at the airport, but it can also be your hotel or holiday accommodation if that suits your itinerary better.
5. **The car is delivered to you.** A representative from the local operator meets you with the car at the agreed time and place, checks your documents, and hands over the keys.
6. **You drive off, no depot, no shuttle.**
7. **At the end of your hire, you return the car** as agreed (often the same meet and greet arrangement in reverse), and any unused portion of your fuel deposit is refunded.

The whole point is that everything happening at steps 1 to 3, the comparing and choosing, happens before you've even left the UK. By the time you land in Spain, the only thing left to do is walk out of arrivals and find your driver.

## How Is This Different From the Traditional Airport Rental Desk?

At a conventional airport rental desk in Spain, you queue at the counter after a long flight, then often queue again for a shuttle bus to an off-site depot, then queue a third time at the depot itself. Meet and greet removes those steps because the car comes to you instead of you going to the car.

| | Meet and Greet (Camel Global) | Traditional Airport Desk |
|---|---|---|
| Where you collect the car | Arrivals, your hotel, or agreed pickup point | Rental desk, often followed by a depot shuttle |
| Queuing | None for collection | Desk queue, sometimes a shuttle queue too |
| Price visibility | See the price before you book | Often confirmed only at the counter |
| Who provides the car | Local operators competing for your booking | Single company, fixed price |
| Fuel | Refundable deposit, pay only for fuel used | Often a prepaid full tank, unused fuel lost |
| Booking | Online, compare offers in one place | Direct with one company |

## What Should You Bring to Arrivals for a Smooth Handover?

A meet and greet handover in Spain is quick, but it still involves a document check, just like any car hire. Have ready:

- Your UK driving licence (a photocard licence; some operators may also want to see the paper counterpart or check your licence details online).
- Your passport, for identification.
- The booking confirmation from Camel Global, ideally on your phone.
- The payment card used for the fuel deposit, if the operator requires it at handover.

Because you've already agreed the price and terms before flying, the handover itself is short: identity check, a quick look at the car together, keys handed over, and you're on your way.

## Is Meet and Greet Car Hire Worth It for a Spain Trip?

It's worth considering if any of these apply to your trip:

- You're landing late or after a long flight and don't want to spend your first hour in Spain queuing.
- You're travelling with young children and want to avoid a depot shuttle bus with luggage and car seats in tow.
- You want to compare prices from more than one local company rather than accepting whatever one desk quotes you.
- You'd rather pay for the fuel you actually use than buy a full tank you won't finish.

If your flight lands early in the day with no connections to make and you're happy to spend time at a desk comparing walk-up prices, the time saving matters less. For most UK holidaymakers arriving in Spain, though, the desk and depot stage is the most tiring part of the whole travel day, and it's the part meet and greet removes.

## What About Fuel, Deposits and Hidden Fees?

Camel Global's fuel arrangement is straightforward: you pay a refundable fuel deposit alongside your hire cost, and when you return the car, you're refunded everything except the fuel you actually used. You're not committing to buy a full tank upfront and losing what you don't burn.

Because you see and compare offers from local operators before you book, there's no last-minute upsell at a counter. The price you agree online is the price you pay through the platform, and your VAT invoice for the hire is issued by the local operator who supplies the car (the booking receipt itself isn't a VAT invoice, so keep the invoice from the operator if you need one for expenses).

## What Happens If My Flight to Spain Is Delayed?

Flight delays are common on routes into Spain, especially in peak summer months. Because your handover is arranged directly with the local operator at a time linked to your actual arrival, it's worth contacting them as soon as you know your flight is delayed so the meet and greet time can be adjusted. This is one of the practical advantages of dealing with a named local operator rather than a queue at a fixed desk: the handover is built around your booking, not a rigid rota.

## Which Airports in Spain Support Car Hire Delivery?

Meet and greet car hire delivery is commonly requested at Spain's busiest UK arrival points, including Malaga, Alicante, Palma de Mallorca, Barcelona, Madrid and Girona. Coverage depends on which local operators are bidding in that area for your specific dates, so it's worth submitting your request with your exact airport and dates to see which companies respond.

## What If I Need a Family Car, Automatic, or One-Way Hire?

When you submit your request on Camel Global, you specify what you need, whether that's an automatic for drivers who aren't confident with a manual on Spanish roads, a larger family car with child seats fitted, or a one-way hire if you're flying into one Spanish city and out of another. Local operators then bid based on what they can actually provide for those requirements, so you're comparing offers that match your trip rather than a generic price list.
