## Driving from Valencia Airport

Once you're behind the wheel, Valencia's road network is generally easy for UK drivers to adapt to, though the obvious adjustment is driving on the right. From the airport, the A-3 and V-30 ring road connect quickly to the city centre, the AP-7 motorway (a toll road), and routes north towards Sagunto and the Costa del Azahar or south towards Cullera and the Costa Blanca.

A few practical points worth knowing before you set off:

- Spanish motorways are generally well-surfaced and clearly signed, but the AP-7 is a toll (peaje) road, while the A-7 running parallel is toll-free and slower.
- Valencia's city centre has restricted low-emission zones in parts of the old town, so if your base is central, it's worth checking whether your accommodation has nearby parking rather than relying on street spaces.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, and speed cameras are common on both motorways and approach roads, so it's worth keeping an eye on signage rather than instinct.
- If you're heading up the coast towards Castellon and the Costa del Azahar, the N-340 and AP-7 both run close to the shoreline with regular exits for towns like Benicassim and Alcossebre.
