# One Way Car Hire Spain: Can You Pick Up in One City and Drop Off in Another With Camel Global?

Yes. One way car hire in Spain, where you collect your car in one city and leave it in another, is something local operators on the Camel Global marketplace can offer, though not every operator provides it and terms vary by route and distance. Because Camel Global works by letting approved local car hire companies bid for your booking, the way to secure a one-way hire is to state your pickup and drop-off locations clearly in your request so operators who cover that route can send you an offer, including any relocation charge they apply. There is no rental desk involved either way: the car is met and handed to you, and left with the operator's team at the end, wherever you agreed.

## What Exactly Is One-Way Car Hire, and Why Would You Need It in Spain?

One-way car hire simply means your start point and end point are different, for example flying into Malaga and finishing your trip in Madrid, Barcelona or Seville rather than looping back to where you began. UK travellers typically want this for:

- Touring holidays: flying into one city, driving a route through Andalusia or along the coast, and flying home from a different airport.
- Multi-stop trips: combining a city break with a beach stay, such as Barcelona to Valencia or Malaga to Granada and on to Alicante.
- One-directional travel: relocating for work, a longer stay, or moving between a Spanish home and an arrival airport.

Because Camel Global connects you with independent local operators rather than a single national fleet, one-way availability and any relocation fee depend entirely on which companies serve both ends of your route. Always specify both locations when you submit your request so operators can quote accurately.

## How Does Meet & Greet One-Way Car Hire Actually Work, Step by Step?

1. Go to camel-global.com and enter your pickup location (for example, Malaga Airport), your drop-off location (for example, Madrid city centre), your dates, and the type of car you need.
2. Approved local operators who can cover that route see your request and send you their offers, including the hire cost and any one-way or relocation charge.
3. Compare the offers on the platform and choose the one that suits you. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit securely through the platform, which processes payments through Stripe.
4. On arrival, the operator's team meets you with the car at the agreed spot, whether that's the airport kerb, your hotel, or another address. No shuttle bus, no rental desk queue.
5. Drive your route. At the end of the hire, you hand the car back to the operator (or their local partner) at the agreed drop-off city rather than returning to where you started.
6. Once the car is checked in, the operator settles the fuel deposit refund, less the fuel you actually used.

## Which Spanish Airports and Cities Work Well for a One-Way Route?

Spain's spread of major airports makes several one-way combinations practical for UK travellers, subject to which operators cover each leg:

- Malaga (Costa del Sol) to Seville, Granada or Madrid: a common route for combining beach time with inland cities.
- Barcelona to Valencia or Alicante: a coastal drive down the Mediterranean.
- Madrid to Bilbao or Santander: for travellers heading north into Basque Country or Cantabria.
- Alicante to Madrid: linking the Costa Blanca with the capital.

Because offers come from local operators bidding individually, not every combination will always have a match. If your exact route doesn't attract offers, try adjusting the drop-off to a nearby city or airport, which often opens up more operator coverage.

## What Does One-Way Hire Cost, and How Do You Avoid Hidden Fees?

Because operators set and compete on their own prices, there is no fixed one-way surcharge to quote here, and Camel Global does not set or guarantee prices. What you can control is transparency:

- State both your pickup and drop-off locations upfront so any relocation charge is included in the offer you see, not added later.
- Compare offers side by side on the platform before booking, so you know exactly what you're paying before you commit.
- Ask about the car's condition report and the fuel deposit amount as part of the offer, so there are no surprises at handover.
- Keep your booking receipt and request the operator's VAT invoice for your hire records, since the booking receipt itself is not a VAT invoice.
- Use free cancellation, available up to 48 hours before pickup, if your plans or route change after booking.

## What Happens to the Fuel Deposit on a One-Way Trip?

You leave a refundable fuel deposit when you book, rather than prepaying for a full tank you might not use. When you hand the car back at your drop-off city, the operator checks the fuel level and refunds the deposit balance based on what you've used. This works the same way whether you're returning the car to your pickup city or dropping it off somewhere else entirely, since the fuel check happens at handback, not at a fixed depot.

## What If My Flight Is Delayed on Pickup Day?

Because your car is delivered to you rather than sitting in a depot bay, communicate any delay to the operator as soon as you know your new arrival time, ideally through the contact details on your booking confirmation. Meet & greet handovers are arranged around your actual arrival, which is one of the practical advantages over a fixed rental desk slot: there's a person meeting you, not an unattended booth you might miss the cut-off for.

## Can I Get an Automatic, a Family Car, or a Longer-Term Hire on a One-Way Route?

When you submit your request, specify the car type you need, automatic transmission, child seats, extra luggage space, or a longer hire period, and operators bidding on your route will reflect that in their offers. UK drivers often prefer automatics for unfamiliar Spanish roads and roundabouts, and families travelling with young children should request child seats directly in the booking rather than assuming they're included. Longer-term one-way hires, for example a two or three week trip finishing in a different city, work the same way: state your full dates and route so operators can quote accordingly.

## Is Meet & Greet One-Way Hire Worth It Compared With the Airport Desk?

For UK travellers doing a touring holiday rather than a simple return trip, one-way meet & greet hire removes two of the most common frustrations: queueing at a desk after a long flight, and being tied to flying home from the same airport you landed at. You compare offers from local operators before you commit, the car is delivered to you rather than the other way round, and you deal with a refundable fuel deposit instead of prepaying for fuel you won't use. Whether it's worth any relocation charge an operator applies depends on your route and how much time and hassle a return trip to your starting airport would otherwise cost you.

### What's the difference between meet & greet and a traditional airport rental desk?

With meet & greet, the car is brought to you at the airport, your hotel, or another agreed location, and handed over in person, so there's no queue at a counter and no shuttle bus to a depot. With a traditional rental desk, you typically queue to check in, then travel to a separate lot to collect the car.

### Can I cancel my one-way booking if my travel plans change?

Yes, Camel Global bookings include free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled pickup time, which is useful if your route or dates shift after you've compared and booked an offer.

### How do I make sure there are no hidden fees on a one-way hire?
