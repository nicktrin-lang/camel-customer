## What should UK drivers know about driving in and around Seville?

Spain drives on the right, which takes most UK drivers a short adjustment period, especially at roundabouts where you give way to the left instead of the right. A few local specifics worth knowing before you set off from Seville:

- **Seville's low emission zone (ZBE)** covers much of the historic centre. Check your hire car's environmental sticker and route before driving into the old town.
- **The historic centre (Casco Antiguo)**, including areas near the Cathedral and Santa Cruz, has narrow streets and restricted vehicle access, so a smaller car is genuinely easier to park and manoeuvre.
- **The SE-30 ring road** loops around the city and is the quickest way to bypass the centre when heading to the A-4 (Córdoba/Madrid direction) or AP-4 (Cádiz, a toll motorway).
- **On-street parking** in central Seville uses blue and green zone bays with paid tickets during the day; many hotels don't have their own car parks, so check parking arrangements when you book accommodation.
- **Speed limits** are generally 50km/h in built-up areas, 90-100km/h on ordinary roads, and 120km/h on motorways unless signed otherwise.
