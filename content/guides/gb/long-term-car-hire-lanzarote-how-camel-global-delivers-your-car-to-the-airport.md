# Long Term Car Hire Lanzarote: How Camel Global Delivers Your Car to the Airport

Long term car hire in Lanzarote means booking a car for an extended stay, typically two weeks or more, rather than a standard week-long holiday rental. With Camel Global, you compare offers from local Lanzarote car hire companies through one platform, choose the deal that suits your dates and budget, and have the car met and handed to you at Lanzarote Airport or your accommodation instead of queuing at a rental desk. ## What Counts as Long Term Car Hire in Lanzarote?

Long term car hire generally covers bookings from around two weeks up to several months. UK travellers use it for a few common reasons:

- Winter sun stays of three to eight weeks, common among retired UK visitors who base themselves in Puerto del Carmen, Playa Blanca or Costa Teguise over autumn and winter.
- Remote workers and digital nomads staying a month or more, who want a car for grocery runs, coworking spaces and weekend exploring rather than daily short rentals.
- Property owners or repeat visitors who return to the same villa or apartment each year and prefer one continuous booking over several short ones.

Because Lanzarote is a relatively compact island (around 60km north to south), a long term hire car is genuinely useful even if you're staying mostly in one resort, since public transport between towns is limited and taxis for regular trips add up quickly over several weeks.

## How Does Meet & Greet Car Hire Work at Lanzarote Airport?

Lanzarote Airport (officially César Manrique-Lanzarote Airport, code ACE) sits just south of Arrecife, a short drive from Puerto del Carmen and around 25 to 40 minutes from Playa Blanca or Costa Teguise. With meet & greet car hire through Camel Global, the process runs like this:

1. You enter your dates, destination and car requirements on camel-global.com.
2. Approved local operators send you competing offers for your booking.
3. You choose an offer and book, paying the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit through the platform.
4. A representative from the local operator meets you at the airport (or your hotel) with the car at the agreed time.
5. You drive off straight away, no shuttle bus to an off-site depot.
6. On return, you hand the car back at the agreed point and the platform manages the refund of any unused portion of your fuel deposit.

This matters more on a long term booking than a short one, because you're not repeating a desk queue or depot transfer at the start of a multi-week trip; you get on the road immediately after a long flight.

## What Are the Roads Like in Lanzarote for a Long Stay?

Lanzarote's road network is well maintained but distinct from the UK in a few ways worth knowing before a long term hire:

- **Driving is on the right.** This takes most UK drivers a day or two to adjust to, particularly at roundabouts, which are common.
- **LZ-2 and LZ-1 are the main dual carriageways**, connecting the airport, Arrecife, Puerto del Carmen and the north of the island. Speeds are moderate and well signposted.
- **Coastal winds can be strong**, especially around Famara and the north coast, so keep a firm grip at higher speeds on exposed stretches.
- **Volcanic terrain roads**, such as those through Timanfaya National Park, are narrow with sharp bends; take these slowly.
- **Village centres like Teguise and Haría** have narrow, sometimes cobbled streets, so a smaller car is easier to manoeuvre if you're staying long enough to explore inland regularly.
- **Fuel stations** are spread around the island but sparser inland, so it's worth knowing your nearest station if you're based away from the main resorts for weeks at a time.

## Best Day Trips from Lanzarote With a Long Term Hire Car

A long term hire car pays for itself in flexibility once you start exploring beyond your resort. Popular routes for UK visitors include:

- **Timanfaya National Park**, the island's volcanic centrepiece, roughly 30 to 40 minutes from most southern resorts.
- **Jameos del Agua and Cueva de los Verdes**, César Manrique's lava tube attractions on the north-east coast.
- **Órzola**, the departure point for the ferry to La Graciosa, a car-free island ideal for a day trip.
- **La Geria wine region**, where vines grow in volcanic ash pits, a short drive from Playa Blanca or Puerto del Carmen.
- **Teguise**, the former capital, known for its Sunday market and historic centre.
- **Famara**, a favourite for surfers and long sandy walks, on the wilder north-west coast.

With a two-, four- or eight-week hire, you can space these out at a relaxed pace rather than cramming them into one holiday week.

## How Do I Avoid Hidden Fees on a Long Term Booking?

- Read the offer details from each operator before booking so you know exactly what's included over the full hire period.
- Confirm the fuel deposit amount and how the refund process works before you travel.
- Ask the local operator directly about mileage limits if you plan to drive extensively over several weeks.
- Keep your booking confirmation and request a proper invoice from the operator for your records, particularly useful if you're claiming expenses or need documentation for a long stay.
- Use the platform's free cancellation window (up to 48 hours before pickup) if your travel dates shift, rather than losing money on a fixed booking made elsewhere.

## What About Fuel on a Long Term Hire?

## Family Car Hire and Child Seats in Lanzarote

If you're staying long term with children, request child seats as part of your booking request so operators can quote accordingly. Family-sized cars are widely available among local Lanzarote operators, and since you're comparing multiple offers rather than one fixed rental company, it's worth specifying seat sizes (infant, toddler, booster) clearly at the request stage to get accurate offers.

## What Happens if My Flight is Delayed?

Because the car is met and handed to you rather than collected from a fixed-hours desk, it's important to communicate any flight delay to the local operator as soon as you know about it, ideally through the contact details provided after booking. Meet & greet handover is built around your actual arrival time, so keeping the operator informed helps them plan the pickup accurately, especially important on a long term booking where the start date sets the whole rental period.
