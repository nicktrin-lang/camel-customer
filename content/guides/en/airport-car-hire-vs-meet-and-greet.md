# Airport Car Hire vs Meet & Greet: Which Is Better?

Both get you a car. Only one gets you out of the airport fast. Here's the honest comparison.

## Time

Traditional desks mean queueing twice — once to collect, once to return. Meet & greet is a handover at the kerb. **Winner: meet & greet.**

## Cost

Desk rentals lure you with low headline rates, then add insurance and fuel policies. Meet & greet bids are all-in and transparent. **Winner: meet & greet.**

## When a desk still makes sense

If you're renting for a month and want a specific chain's loyalty points, a desk may suit you. For a week's holiday, meet & greet wins on every axis.
