## What should UK drivers know about driving in and around Valencia?

Spain drives on the right, which is the single biggest adjustment for UK drivers, particularly at roundabouts and when overtaking. A few practical points:

- **Documents**: bring your UK photocard driving licence and passport. Check current requirements for an International Driving Permit before you travel, as rules can change.
- **Tolls**: the AP-7 coastal motorway and some routes north and south of Valencia are toll roads (autopistas de peaje); the free A-7 and N-332 run parallel but are slower.
- **Low emission zone**: Valencia operates a Zona de Bajas Emisiones (ZBE) covering parts of the city centre, with restrictions that can affect older vehicles. Check with your operator whether this affects your route.
- **Parking**: much of the historic centre (Ciutat Vella) is limited traffic access for residents; blue-zone (ORA) paid parking applies in many other central streets.
- **Speed limits**: generally 50km/h in urban areas, 90-100km/h on conventional roads, and 120km/h on motorways, though signage always takes priority.

## Best day trips from Valencia with your hire car

Having a car delivered to your hotel opens up the wider Valencia region well beyond the tram and metro network:

- **Albufera Natural Park**: around 30 minutes south, famous for its lagoon, rice paddies and the birthplace of paella valenciana.
- **Xàtiva**: an inland hill town about an hour away, with a hilltop castle and Roman and Moorish history.
- **Sagunto**: 30 minutes north, with a Roman theatre and castle overlooking the coast.
- **Requena wine region**: roughly an hour inland, known for bodegas and vineyard visits.
- **Peñíscola**: around 1.5 hours north up the coast, with its walled old town and castle.

None of these are easily reached without your own transport on a tight schedule, which is where a delivered hire car pays off.

## What about fuel, deposits and hidden fees?

Camel Global's fuel policy is straightforward: you leave a refundable fuel deposit when you book, and once the car is returned, the amount corresponding to fuel actually used is deducted, with the rest refunded. There's no requirement to return the car with a full tank you may not have used, and no obligation to prepay for fuel you won't burn. To avoid hidden car hire fees generally, always check what's included in an operator's offer, insurance excess, mileage limits and any additional driver costs, before accepting a bid. Because offers are shown transparently through the platform, you can compare these details before you book rather than discovering them at the desk.
