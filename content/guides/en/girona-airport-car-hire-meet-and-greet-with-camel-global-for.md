Once you have your car, a little preparation goes a long way. Remember Spain drives on the right, which takes most UK drivers a short while to adjust to, particularly at roundabouts. Your full UK driving licence is valid for hire and driving in Spain, and it is worth carrying your passport and booking confirmation with you when you collect the car. The AP-7 motorway running along the coast is a toll road and a fast way to reach towns like Tossa de Mar, Palamós, or Begur, while the older N-II and local coastal roads are slower but scenic, if you have time to enjoy the drive. Parking in the old towns of Girona, Pals, or Cadaqués can be tight in peak season, so many visitors prefer to park on the outskirts and walk in.

If your itinerary includes both the coast and inland towns such as Girona city itself, with its cathedral and old Jewish quarter, having your own car from the moment you land makes the whole trip far more flexible than relying on transfers or local buses timed around flight schedules.
