## Driving From Reus to PortAventura and the Coast

Once you've collected your car, the drive itself is short and manageable. PortAventura and the main Costa Dorada resorts sit just off the AP-7 motorway, a toll road that runs down the coast, with Salou and Cambrils only a few minutes further on. Signage is clear and in Spanish and Catalan, and the roads around Reus and Tarragona province are generally well maintained.

A few things UK drivers should keep in mind:

- Spain drives on the right, which takes a little concentration at roundabouts and junctions for the first hour or so, particularly if your hire car is left-hand drive as most local cars will be.
- Motorway tolls are common on the AP-7. Keep some coins or a card handy at toll booths, or check with your operator whether an electronic tag is fitted.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, not miles, and are lower in built-up areas around Salou and Cambrils in high season.
- Parking in central Salou and Cambrils can be tight in summer. PortAventura itself has large dedicated car parks for visitors.
- You'll need your UK driving licence and, depending on current requirements, it's worth checking whether an International Driving Permit is needed at the time you travel.
