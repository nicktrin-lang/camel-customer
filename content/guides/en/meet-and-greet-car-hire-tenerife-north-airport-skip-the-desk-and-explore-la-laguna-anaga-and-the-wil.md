# Meet and Greet Car Hire Tenerife North Airport: Camel Global Delivers Your Car to Los Rodeos

Most UK travellers heading to Tenerife land at the sprawling southern airport and never see the island's older, quieter face. But if your flight touches down at Tenerife North, known locally as Los Rodeos or Tenerife Norte, you're already closer to the parts of the island that package holidays tend to skip: the UNESCO-listed old town of La Laguna, the laurel forests of the Anaga Mountains, and a north coast of black sand, cliffs and small fishing towns that feels a world away from the southern resorts. Getting a hire car delivered to you at arrivals, rather than queuing at a rental desk, is a genuinely sensible way to start that kind of trip.

## Why Tenerife North Is a Different Kind of Arrival

Tenerife North airport is smaller and simpler than its southern counterpart, which is part of its appeal. It mainly handles inter-island flights and a good number of routes from mainland Spain, though some UK and European charters and connections also use it, particularly for travellers heading straight into the north of the island rather than the resort belt around Costa Adeje and Los Cristianos. The single terminal building is compact, arrivals and baggage reclaim are close together, and there's rarely the long, tired trudge you get at bigger hubs. That compactness matters for meet and greet car hire: a short, clearly signed arrivals area makes it easy to agree a simple pickup point with your driver, whether that's just outside the terminal doors or in the adjacent short-stay area.

The airport sits close to La Laguna itself, only a few minutes' drive along the TF-5, and around 20 to 25 minutes from Santa Cruz de Tenerife, the island's capital. That proximity is exactly why the traditional rental desk model makes little sense here. Why collect a car from a depot, hunt for the exit, and fight rush-hour traffic on unfamiliar roads, when the car can simply be waiting for you at arrivals, ready to drive straight into La Laguna's old streets or up towards Anaga within minutes of landing?

## Driving Out of Tenerife North: What to Expect

The roads immediately around the airport are well maintained dual carriageway, and the TF-5 motorway connects quickly to Santa Cruz, La Laguna and the north coast towns of Bajamar and Punta del Hidalgo. Where driving gets more interesting is once you leave the main routes. The Anaga Mountains are laced with narrow, twisting mountain roads, many single track with passing places, wrapped in cloud forest and offering viewpoints that plunge straight down to the sea. These roads reward a car that isn't oversized, and a driver who takes bends slowly and expects the occasional local car, cyclist or hiker around the next corner. La Laguna's old town has narrow, one-way cobbled streets and limited on-street parking, so many visitors leave the car on the edge of the historic centre and explore on foot, which is easily done given how walkable it is.
