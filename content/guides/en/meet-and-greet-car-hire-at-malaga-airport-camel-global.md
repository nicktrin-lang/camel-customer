## Why This Matters at Malaga Specifically

Malaga is one of the busiest airports on the Spanish coast, particularly through the spring and summer months when flights from the UK land in clusters throughout the day. Terminal 3 handles the vast majority of international arrivals, and on a busy afternoon the walk from passport control to the rental car area, followed by the wait for a shuttle bus to an off-site depot, can easily eat 45 minutes to an hour of your holiday before you've even left the airport perimeter. For a family with young children, or anyone who's booked an early check-in at a hotel along the Costa del Sol, that's time better spent on the road — or on the beach.
