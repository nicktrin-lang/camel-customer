That drive is exactly where meet & greet delivery earns its keep: instead of finding an off-site depot after a long flight, your car can be waiting for you, or delivered later to your hotel if you'd rather taxi in first and start driving the next day.

## How does meet and greet car hire work in Benidorm?

The process is designed to remove the two most tiring parts of arrival: the rental desk queue and the depot transfer.

1. Tell Camel Global your destination (Benidorm or Alicante Airport), dates, and the type of car you need, including any extras like child seats.
2. Approved local car hire companies in the Alicante/Benidorm area send you their offers.
3. You compare and pick the offer that suits you, then book. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit.
4. The operator meets you at the agreed time and place, whether that's the arrivals area at Alicante Airport or your hotel entrance in Benidorm.
5. You drive off straight away, no shuttle bus, no depot visit.
6. When you return the car, you're refunded everything from your fuel deposit except the fuel you actually used.

This is different from booking with a big chain where the car is usually only available from a desk inside or near the terminal, often followed by a walk or bus ride to a compound.

## What should UK drivers know about driving from Alicante Airport to Benidorm?

- Spain drives on the right, which takes most UK drivers a short adjustment period, especially at roundabouts and when pulling out of the airport car park.
- The main route is the AP-7 toll motorway or the free N-332, both running north from Alicante past Villajoyosa into Benidorm; the N-332 is slower but avoids tolls.
- Speed limits are in kilometres per hour, not miles, so check your speedometer display or set it to km/h before you leave.
- Spanish law requires a reflective vest kept inside the car (not the boot) and a warning triangle, and dipped headlights are compulsory in tunnels.
- The legal blood alcohol limit in Spain is lower than in the UK, so it's simplest to avoid drinking entirely if you're driving that day.
- Benidorm's Poniente and Levante beach zones get busy with pedestrians and one-way systems in high season; use a signed car park rather than searching for street parking near the seafront.

### Do I need to hire a car in Benidorm, or is public transport enough?
Benidorm's centre, beaches and hotel strip are walkable, and buses connect nearby towns, but a hire car is the practical option if you want to reach Guadalest, Calpe, Altea or Alicante city on your own schedule rather than waiting on excursion coaches.

### How far is Alicante Airport from Benidorm?
Alicante Airport is around 50km from Benidorm, typically a 40 to 50 minute drive via the AP-7 motorway or the toll-free N-332, depending on traffic and your exact hotel location.

### Can I get the car delivered to my hotel instead of the airport?
Yes. With Camel Global you agree the delivery point when you book, so the operator can meet you at Alicante Airport arrivals or bring the car directly to your hotel in Benidorm if you'd rather settle in first.

### What happens to my fuel deposit if I don't use a full tank?
You pay a refundable fuel deposit when you book. When you return the car, you're refunded the deposit minus the value of the fuel you actually used, so you're not paying for a full tank you never burned.
