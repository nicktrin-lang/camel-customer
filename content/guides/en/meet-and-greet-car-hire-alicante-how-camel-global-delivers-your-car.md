# Meet and Greet Car Hire Alicante: How Camel Global Delivers Your Car

Meet and greet car hire in Alicante means your hire car is brought to you at Alicante-Elche Airport (or your hotel) instead of you queuing at a rental desk after landing. With Camel Global, you tell us your dates and car type, local Alicante operators send competitive offers, you pick one and book online, and a driver hands the keys over at the agreed time and place. You pay the hire cost plus a refundable fuel deposit, and get back what you didn't use once the car is returned.

For UK travellers flying into Alicante, this matters more than at most airports: arrivals halls can be busy through the peak summer season, and the traditional rental car area sits a short walk from the terminal, often with several counters and long queues after every wave of flights lands. Meet and greet skips that entirely.

## How does meet and greet car hire work at Alicante Airport?

The process runs in five simple steps:

1. **Request your car.** On camel-global.com, enter your Alicante dates, the type of vehicle you need, and where you want it delivered, whether that's arrivals at Alicante-Elche Airport, a hotel in Benidorm or Alicante city, or a villa along the Costa Blanca.
2. **Local operators bid.** Approved car hire companies operating in the Alicante area send you their offers, so you can compare price and vehicle side by side.
3. **Choose and book.** Pick the offer that suits you and pay through the platform. Payments are processed through Stripe, and the platform supports multiple currencies and six languages, which is useful given how many nationalities pass through Alicante-Elche.
4. **Meet your car.** The operator's representative meets you at the agreed spot with the vehicle, checks your documents, and hands over the keys.
5. **Drive off, and settle up on return.** At the end of your hire, you pay for the fuel you actually used from your deposit, and the rest is refunded.

There's no shuttle bus to an off-site depot and no standing in a queue behind a dozen other tired arrivals.

## Driving from Alicante Airport: what UK visitors should know

Alicante-Elche Airport sits south-west of Alicante city, close to the AP-7 motorway, which runs along the Costa Blanca connecting Alicante to Benidorm, Denia, and south towards Torrevieja and Murcia. A few points UK drivers should keep in mind:

- **Drive on the right.** This is the biggest adjustment for UK drivers, particularly at roundabouts and when overtaking.
- **Tolls apply on the AP-7** in sections, while the parallel N-332 is toll-free but slower and passes through more towns.
- **Speed limits** are in kilometres per hour: typically 120 km/h on motorways, 90 to 100 km/h on main roads, and 50 km/h in built-up areas.
- **A UK driving licence** is valid for driving in Spain; carry it along with your passport and hire documents.
- **Parking in town centres** such as Alicante's Casco Antiguo or central Benidorm is often metered or restricted, so check your hotel's parking arrangement in advance.

## What are the best day trips from Alicante with a hire car?

Having your own car opens up the Costa Blanca well beyond the resort strip:

- **Guadalest**, a mountain village around 45 minutes inland, known for its clifftop castle and reservoir views.
- **Elche**, roughly 20 minutes from the airport, home to Europe's largest palm grove and a UNESCO-listed historic centre.
- **Altea and Calpe**, further up the coast, with the dramatic Peñón de Ifach rock and a picturesque old town.
- **Tabarca Island**, reached by boat from Alicante or Santa Pola, a good day out if you park up and take the crossing.
- **Murcia city**, around an hour south, for those wanting a change from the coast.

A delivered car means you can start these trips straight from your accommodation rather than first collecting the vehicle from a depot.
